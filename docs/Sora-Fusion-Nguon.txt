SORA Fusion - THONG TIN NGUON VA QUYEN SU DUNG

Nguon do nguoi dung cung cap (ba ZIP):
- lachydotmcg/capri-desktop: Source for 60-frame perch and music-perch sheets.
- capridotcom/Capri-AI: C# reference implementation of Windows Media Sessions, Mainframe and Agent.
- mochiiwuff/capri-desktop: Original RT Electron animation and desktop companion architecture.

Tep duoc nhap tu tai lieu nguon:
  assets/sora/sora-perch-60.png (original sprites/perch.png)
  assets/sora/sora-perch-music-60.png (original sprites/perch_music.png)

Cac file anh la tac pham do nghe si cua du an goc cung cap, co the KHONG duoc cap
phep thong qua giay phep ma nguon. Chi su dung noi bo va phi thuong mai neu
ban co quyen su dung; khong tu dong tai su dung/phan phoi lai ngoai pham vi quyen.
Phan code cua lich su Capri co nhieu phien ban va giay phep khac nhau;
khong thay doi/cat bo cac thong bao ban quyen hoac danh tinh nguoi dong gop.

Ma backend Fusion va bo khung mini-player: trien khai moi cho Sora,
khong khoi chay bo nao C# rieng hay sao chep nghiem ngat mot Mainframe cua Capri.
Windows Media Bridge (C#) la tinh nang tuy chon; can .NET 8 SDK va
Microsoft.Windows.SDK.Contracts de bien dich. SORA van chay khi khong co.

Khong nhap cac WAV giong noi tu du an goc, khong clone giong dien vien.
Speech-to-text: su dung faster-whisper tai cho, phai cai thu vien va model tuong ung.
Text-to-speech: Piper chi co the doc tieng Viet khi nguoi dung cung cap
Piper binary va model tieng Viet hop le (SORA_PIPER_EXE, SORA_PIPER_VOICE).
Micro luon mac dinh TAT. Khong gui anh man hinh hay micro len cloud mac dinh.
