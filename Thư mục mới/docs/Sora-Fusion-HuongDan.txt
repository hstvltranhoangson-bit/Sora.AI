SORA FUSION — HUONG DAN (duy tri README.md duy nhat)

1. Dong hoan toan SORA.
2. Giai nen goi vao F:\Sora.AI, chon GHI DE file cai dat neu co.
3. PowerShell:
   powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File F:\Sora.AI\CaiFusion-Sora.ps1 -Preview
   powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File F:\Sora.AI\CaiFusion-Sora.ps1
4. Mo Sora.exe, nhap phai icon tray > 'Sora Fusion'.

VOICE (tieng Viet):
- Bat dau noi / Ket thuc: Micro OFF neu khong nhan nut.
- Hey Sora OFF mac dinh; chi bat sau khi dong y. No nghe microphone
  va chi gui cau len Sora sau khi nhan dien tu khoa, khong luu WAV.
- Can pip install sounddevice numpy faster-whisper neu may chua co.
- Whisper model lan dau co the can tai, sau do cache tai may.
- TTS chi hoat dong neu ban co Piper EXE va mo hinh giong Viet
  ma ban co quyen dung; khai bao SORA_PIPER_EXE, SORA_PIPER_VOICE.
- Khong copy WAV tieng Anh hay giong dien vien tu Capri de doc tieng Viet.

NHAC:
- Mini-player co nut truoc, phat/tam dung, tiep, am.
- Tinh nang doc ten bai nhac theo phien Windows chi co khi bien dich
  SORA\tools\SoraMedia\SoraMedia.exe. Chay Build-SoraMedia.ps1 sau khi
  da cai .NET 8 SDK va co ket noi NuGet.
- Truoc khi co SoraMedia.exe, nhan nut van gui media keys; ten bai, trang thai
  phat khong tu dong xuat hien. Co che do xem truoc hoat anh nhac rieng.
- Sprite 60 khung (12 fps), su dung tai tu lieu Capri ma ban da cung cap.
  Neu thay khong hop nhan vat, bo chon trong bang dieu khien.

MODEL AI:
- Chi chuyen qua model Ollama da cai, khong tac dong SQLite hoc hoi.
- Sau khi chuyen model, Sora su dung cho cac luot chat tiep theo.

AGENT / KE HOACH:
- Mot muc tieu 1-20 buoc; duyet ke hoach truoc.
- Chi tim ten file trong Desktop/Documents/Downloads/Pictures; khong doc noi dung.
- Ket qua moi buoc phai co bang chung nguoi dung tu xac nhan.
- Esc trong bang dieu khien ngat ke hoach; Esc khi Agent chat dang chay
  gui lenh huy yeu cau dang chay. Khong tu chay script, xoa file hay dong app.
- Dau ra bao cao = buoc da hoan thanh + bang chung da nhap.
- Khong thay the Desktop Agent Core cua Sora, khong tu dong hoa vo kiem soat.

Luu y: Nha cung cap nhieu AI (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini...) KHONG duoc dung
  de song song chat voi Sora. Hien tai chuyen MODEL noi bo Ollama, cac nha
  cung cap khac co the duoc ho tro trong tuong lai sau khi khoa API an toan.

NOI DUNG CHUA CO: Khong co bo danh gia nhip am thanh BPM chinh xac,
  khong co wakeword engine nhan dang theo dac trung giong noi,
  khong co Desktop Agent tu chay cac buoc tu dong khong can nguoi duyet.
